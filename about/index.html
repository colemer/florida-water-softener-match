About Florida Water Softener Match

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Water Softener Match

Florida Water Softener Match is a free referral and matching service. We are not a water treatment company or contractor, we do not send crews, and we do not perform any water softener and filtration or construction work.

How it works

  1. You submit a request describing the problem.
  2. We review it. We aim to review requests within a few hours during the day, every day. Overnight requests are reviewed the next morning.
  3. No partner companies are listed yet, so we do not share any request with any company. We (Cole Merlino, Florida Water Softener Match) read your request and reply to you ourselves, by email (or by phone or text only if you checked the box). A company added later will never receive a request sent before it was named here. When partners are listed, they are named in the consent box on the form and on our partners page, up to 3 per request.
  4. You decide whether to hire anyone. Pricing, scheduling and the work itself are between you and the contractor.

How we're paid

Participating companies may pay us a fee to advertise on this site or to receive requests. You never pay us, and it does not change what you are quoted.

What we check, and what we don't

Before we list a partner, we look the company up on the Florida DBPR license search and show the license number we found, and the date we checked, next to its name on our partners page (or note that its work has no state license). That is the only check we do. We do not inspect work, check insurance, run background checks, or guarantee any company's work, pricing or availability. Licenses can change, so check DBPR yourself before you hire.

Our content

Our local pages and guides cite official sources (NOAA, FEMA, Florida statutes, county and city building departments) and say so when a figure is a national estimate rather than local data. If you spot an error, tell us through the request form and we'll correct it.

Who we are

Florida Water Softener Match is operated by Cole Merlino, Florida. Contact: the request form on this site (write "Privacy" or "Correction" in the details box). We have no physical office open to the public and are not affiliated with any contractor.

Florida Water Softener Match is a free referral service, not a contractor. We do not perform water softener and filtration or any construction work. With the homeowner’s consent, we pass a request to up to 3 independent local companies named on our form (none are listed yet, so no requests are shared). Participating companies may pay us a fee to advertise on this site or to receive requests. You never pay us, and it does not change what you are quoted. Each contractor is responsible for its own licensing, insurance, pricing and work. You are never obligated to hire anyone.
