Hard water in Atlantic Beach & Neptune Beach: what's different here

Atlantic Beach is one of the few local utilities that publishes a hardness number specifically for setting a softener: 18 grains per gallon. Neptune Beach next door runs its own water service, so the two cities need separate numbers.

  • Atlantic Beach publishes its hardness for softener settings: 300 milligrams per liter, or 18 grains per gallon.[5]
  • Neptune Beach runs its own water service (utility billing 904-270-2400, ext. 25), so Neptune Beach homes should use the city's water quality report or a tap test rather than Atlantic Beach's figure.[6]

Who issues permits in Atlantic Beach & Neptune Beach?

Atlantic Beach Building Division / Neptune Beach Building Department. Phone: Atlantic Beach 904-247-5800; Neptune Beach 904-270-2400. Each city issues its own permits. Atlantic Beach accepts applications online; Neptune Beach's building department is at 116 First St. (permits 904-270-2400, inspections 904-270-2430). Neither is the City of Jacksonville.[7][8]

Florida law exempts installation and maintenance of water conditioning units by water conditioning operators from contractor and plumber licensing, but separate plumbing or electrical work (for example a new outlet or re-piping) is regulated. Ask the office whether your install needs a permit.

What does it cost?
