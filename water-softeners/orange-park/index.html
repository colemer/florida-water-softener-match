Hard water in Orange Park: what's different here

Orange Park is at the soft end of the region: JEA's nearby ZIPs are listed as hard, not very hard, and the town runs its own four-well system. Some homeowners here may decide filtration alone is enough.

  • The Town of Orange Park's water comes from four wells in the Upper Floridan Aquifer.[4]
  • JEA customers around Orange Park are listed at the soft end of JEA's table: 128 ppm (8 gpg) in 32073 and 120 ppm (7 gpg) in 32065, which JEA classes as hard rather than very hard.[5]

Who issues permits in Orange Park?

Town of Orange Park Economic & Community Development (in town); Clay County Building Division (outside town). Phone: Town 904-264-2635; Clay County 904-284-6307. Many 'Orange Park' mailing addresses, including much of Oakleaf (32065), are outside town limits and are permitted by Clay County, not the town.[6][7]

Florida law exempts installation and maintenance of water conditioning units by water conditioning operators from contractor and plumber licensing, but separate plumbing or electrical work (for example a new outlet or re-piping) is regulated. Ask the office whether your install needs a permit.

What does it cost?

Angi's 2026 cost guide puts the U.S. average for water softener installation at about $1,500, with a reported range of roughly $200 to $6,000 depending on system type, size and plumbing.[8] These are national figures; we have not found public local cost data.

Who supplies tap water in Orange Park?

How hard is the water in Orange Park?

JEA customers around Orange Park are listed at the soft end of JEA's table: 128 ppm (8 gpg) in 32073 and 120 ppm (7 gpg) in 32065, which JEA classes as hard rather than very hard.[5]

Where do permit questions for a softener install in Orange Park go?

Town of Orange Park Economic & Community Development (in town); Clay County Building Division (outside town). Many 'Orange Park' mailing addresses, including much of Oakleaf (32065), are outside town limits and are permitted by Clay County, not the town.[6][7]

Is Orange Park water soft enough to skip a softener?

It is softer than most of the region by JEA's table (7 to 8 grains per gallon nearby), but JEA still classes it as hard. A tap test and what bothers you (spotting, scale, taste) should decide.[5]
