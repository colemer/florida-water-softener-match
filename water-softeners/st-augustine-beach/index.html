Who issues permits in St. Augustine Beach?

City of St. Augustine Beach Building & Zoning. Phone: 904-471-8758. The city runs its own building department; applications and plans are submitted to the city, not St. Johns County. Parts of Anastasia Island outside city limits (Butler Beach, Crescent Beach) are permitted by the county.[5][6]

Florida law exempts installation and maintenance of water conditioning units by water conditioning operators from contractor and plumber licensing, but separate plumbing or electrical work (for example a new outlet or re-piping) is regulated. Ask the office whether your install needs a permit.

What does it cost?

Angi's 2026 cost guide puts the U.S. average for water softener installation at about $1,500, with a reported range of roughly $200 to $6,000 depending on system type, size and plumbing.[7] These are national figures; we have not found public local cost data.
