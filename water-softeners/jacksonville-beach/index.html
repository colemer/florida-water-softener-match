Hard water in Jacksonville Beach: what's different here

Jacksonville Beach runs its own deep-well system rather than buying from JEA, and the city gives its hardness number by phone rather than online. Get that number before you compare quotes.

  • Jacksonville Beach supplies its own water from artesian wells 900 to 1,200 feet into the Floridan Aquifer; its 2025 report describes five wells and aeration at the plant.[4][5]
  • The city does not post a hardness figure; its FAQ tells residents to call the Water Plant Division at 904-247-6278 for the number to set a softener. JEA customers in 32250 are listed at 292 ppm (17 gpg).[4][6]

Who issues permits in Jacksonville Beach?

City of Jacksonville Beach Building Inspection Division. Phone: 904-247-6235. Jacksonville Beach issues its own permits (online E-Permit or in person at 11 N. 3rd St.). It is not part of the consolidated City of Jacksonville.[7]

Florida law exempts installation and maintenance of water conditioning units by water conditioning operators from contractor and plumber licensing, but separate plumbing or electrical work (for example a new outlet or re-piping) is regulated. Ask the office whether your install needs a permit.

What does it cost?

Angi's 2026 cost guide puts the U.S. average for water softener installation at about $1,500, with a reported range of roughly $200 to $6,000 depending on system type, size and plumbing.[8] These are national figures; we have not found public local cost data.
