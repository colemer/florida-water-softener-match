Does a water softener installer need a license in Florida?

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Water Softener Match

Short answer

Short answer: Not for the softener itself. Section 489.103(15), Florida Statutes, exempts the installation and maintenance of water conditioning units by operators of water conditioning services from contractor licensing, and bars cities and counties from requiring those operators to be licensed as plumbers. Other plumbing and electrical work is still regulated.

  2. What it doesn't cover
  3. How we list companies

What the exemption covers

The exemption covers installing and maintaining water conditioning units for domestic, commercial or industrial purposes by operators of water conditioning services.[1]

What it doesn't cover

Work outside the unit itself, such as new plumbing runs or a new electrical outlet, can require a licensed plumber or electrician and a permit. Ask who does that part and whether it is permitted.[1]

How we list companies

Before we list a partner, we look it up on the Florida DBPR license search and show what we found (including that its softener work needs no state license, if so) and the date we checked.[2]
