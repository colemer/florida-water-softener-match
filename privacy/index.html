Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Water Softener Match

Summary: we collect what you type into our form so we can reply to you and, if you agree, connect you with local companies. Participating contractors may pay us for service requests (leads), so sharing your request with a named partner can be a sale of your information to that company. We share it only with the companies named in the consent box when you submit, and only if you check that box. We do not share it with anyone else, including public adjusters. Right now no partner companies are listed, so we share no requests with anyone.

What we collect

  • Form fields: name, phone, email, ZIP, city or area, type of problem and details.
  • Request record: the page URL you submitted from, date and time, your browser's user-agent, whether you checked the consent box, and the exact consent wording shown (version 2026-11-21-v2). Our form processor may log your IP address.
  • Basic, cookie-free traffic statistics if enabled.

How we use and share it

  • To review your request and respond by email.
  • If you checked the consent box: to share your request with the companies named in that box (up to 3, also listed on our partners page). They may pay us for it, and they may call or text you about your request. What they receive: your name, phone, email, ZIP, city or area, the type of problem you chose and your details. A company named later never receives an earlier request.
  • If you did not check the box: we reply by email only and do not share your information with any company.
  • To keep records of consent, to prevent fraud and to comply with law.

Service providers

Form submissions are delivered through a third-party form service (web3forms) and stored in our email. Hosting is provided by a static-site host.

Your choices

  • Leave the consent box unchecked and your request is not shared with any company.
  • Reply STOP to any text to opt out of texts. Tell any caller to stop calling, or tell us through the request form; we pass opt-outs on to any company that received your request.
  • Contact us through the request form on this site (write "Privacy" or "Correction" in the details box) to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted (we may keep consent records as required by law).

Retention

We keep requests and consent records for up to 5 years, then delete them.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13.
