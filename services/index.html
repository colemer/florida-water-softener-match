Hard water services

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Water Softener Match

  • Water Softener Installation: A water softener removes the calcium and magnesium that make water hard, usually by ion exchange.
  • Softener Replacement & Repair: Replacing an old softener at an existing loop is usually simpler than a new install.
  • Whole-House Filtration: Whole-house filters (sediment, carbon) target taste, odor and chlorine or chloramine; they do not soften water by themselves..
  • Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water: Under-sink reverse osmosis (RO) systems treat drinking and cooking water at one tap.
