Softener Replacement & Repair in Florida

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Water Softener Match

Short answer: Replacing an old softener at an existing loop is usually simpler than a new install. Ask for the new unit's capacity in grains and how it was sized to your hardness.[1]

Find your utility's published hardness (or ask for a free tap test) so any quote is sized to your actual water.

What to know

  • Ask whether removal and disposal of the old unit is included.
  • Re-check hardness after install; your utility's published figure is an average.
