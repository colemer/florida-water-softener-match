Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water in Florida

Last updated November 21, 2026 · Written for Florida homeowners by Florida Water Softener Match

Short answer: Under-sink reverse osmosis (RO) systems treat drinking and cooking water at one tap. Some utilities already use RO at the plant: the City of St. Augustine and St. Johns County's CR 214 Mainland plant both list reverse osmosis in their treatment.[1][2]

Find your utility's published hardness (or ask for a free tap test) so any quote is sized to your actual water.

What to know

  • If your utility already uses RO, test your tap before buying more treatment.
  • Point-of-use RO does not protect pipes and appliances from scale; a softener does.
