What happens next

  1. We review your request. We aim to review requests within a few hours during the day, every day. Overnight requests are reviewed the next morning.
  2. Who contacts you. No partner companies are listed yet, so we do not share any request with any company. We (Cole Merlino, Florida Water Softener Match) read your request and reply to you ourselves, by email (or by phone or text only if you checked the box). A company added later will never receive a request sent before it was named here.
  3. You choose. Hire whoever you prefer, or no one.
While you wait: Look at your water bill to see which utility serves you, and note how many people live in the home. Installers size softeners from hardness and daily water use.

Florida Water Softener Match is a free referral service, not a contractor. We do not perform water softener and filtration or any construction work. With the homeowner’s consent, we pass a request to up to 3 independent local companies named on our form (none are listed yet, so no requests are shared). Participating companies may pay us a fee to advertise on this site or to receive requests. You never pay us, and it does not change what you are quoted. Each contractor is responsible for its own licensing, insurance, pricing and work. You are never obligated to hire anyone.
