Frequently asked questions

Is this a water treatment company?

No. Florida Water Softener Match is a free referral service. With your consent, we can pass your request to up to 3 independent local water treatment companies named on our form.

How hard is Northeast Florida water?

Most of it is hard to very hard. JEA's published averages run from about 7 to 22 grains per gallon depending on ZIP code, and Atlantic Beach reports 18 grains per gallon.[1][2]

Does a softener installer need a plumbing license in Florida?

Not for installing or maintaining the water conditioning unit itself, under s. 489.103(15), Florida Statutes. Other plumbing or electrical work is regulated.[3]
