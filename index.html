What to look for in a water treatment company

How our matching works
Check 1 · Licensing

Know Florida's licensing rule

  • Florida exempts water-conditioning installers from contractor licensing, and local governments may not require them to be licensed as plumbers
  • So ask about insurance, years in business and service response times instead
  • Ask for a water test before you get a quote; JEA publishes hardness by ZIP

Frequently asked questions

Is this a water treatment company?

No. Florida Water Softener Match is a free referral service. With your consent, we can pass your request to up to 3 independent local water treatment companies named on our form.

How hard is Northeast Florida water?

Most of it is hard to very hard. JEA's published averages run from about 7 to 22 grains per gallon depending on ZIP code, and Atlantic Beach reports 18 grains per gallon.[2][4]

Does a softener installer need a plumbing license in Florida?

Not for installing or maintaining the water conditioning unit itself, under s. 489.103(15), Florida Statutes. Other plumbing or electrical work is regulated.[1]
